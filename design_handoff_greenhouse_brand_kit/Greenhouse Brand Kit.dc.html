Logo
The jackalope is a one-color stamp — never gradients, never outlines, never more than one per view. Wyoming-born, slightly mythical, entirely serious.

PRINT / LIGHT CONTEXTS
CLEAR SPACE
Keep a margin of ½ the mark's width on all sides. Minimum size: 24px digital, 12mm print.
NEVER
Rotate, outline, or add gradients · recolor outside the palette · pair with a second mascot or emoji · place over busy photography without a scrim.

02
Color
Dark-first. Moss carries ~70% of every screen; chartreuse is rationed to one glowing accent per view. Amber belongs to data, eyebrows, and warnings — never buttons.

page background
BONE #F0EDDF

body text
LICHEN #7A9678
captions, muted labels
PROPORTION
Moss 70 · Fern 20 · Bone 6 · Glow 2.5 · Ember 1.5. If a screen feels loud, remove chartreuse first.

Typography
Three voices: Newsreader speaks, Archivo explains, Plex Mono measures. The italic-chartreuse turn inside a headline is the brand's signature move — use it once per page.
