One-color jackalope logo stamp; use for every appearance of the mark — never an <img> of the raw PNG.

```jsx
<Jackalope size={34} color="var(--glow)" src="../assets/jackalope.png" />
```

Approved colors: var(--glow) on moss (primary), var(--bone) on moss (quiet), var(--moss) on glow/bone (inverse/light). Max one per view. `src` must be the correct relative path from the page.
