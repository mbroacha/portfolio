How to tell if --tracking-meta is wrong

Six copies of the real Sysgit rail. Five are IBM Plex Sans at 12px with different letter-spacing. The last is IBM Plex Mono at its old setting, so you can see what you are trading away.

0.02em is what is in the repo now. Scroll sideways. The reading test is below.

Judge at your normal screen distance, not zoomed in.

What each failure looks like

Too tight (0 to 0.01em). The rail reads as a single gray block instead of separate lines. Your eye lands on the column, not on a line. In squint mode it goes solid. Word shapes in Design Lead, sole designer stop separating and you start decoding rather than reading.

Too loose (0.04em and up). It stops looking like a document and starts looking like UI chrome, the all-caps eyebrow every startup marketing page uses. At 12px the letters begin to disconnect and you read letters instead of words. Short lines in a 33% column also wrap earlier, which opens ragged holes down the right edge of the rail.

Three specific checks

  • Squint at it. Turn on squint mode. You want an even gray column with visible line separation. A solid slab means too tight. A stripey scatter with gaps means too loose.
  • Read the longest value. Product, design system, research, strategy, brand, front-end PRs wraps to three lines. If your eye stumbles at the wrap, or if the second line looks like a different size than the first, the tracking is fighting the measure.
  • Check it against the labels. The uppercase labels already carry 0.08em separately. Uppercase always needs more than lowercase, because capitals have no ascenders or descenders to separate them. If the lowercase values look as spaced out as the labels, you have gone too far.

My read

0.02em is deliberately conservative. If it is wrong it will be wrong in the tight direction, not the loose one, so the only real question is whether to go up to 0.03em. Beyond that you are building a label system, which is the aesthetic the Dark CV is specifically avoiding.

The thing actually doing the work here is not the tracking. It is font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums, which keeps 2023 - 2026 and the team counts aligned down the rail. That was the only thing the monospaced face was genuinely earning, and Plex Sans has it built in. Compare the last two columns and you will see the dates behave the same.

The knob is --tracking-meta in src/styles/tokens.css.
