#244233. Hue 150, so it sits just off pure green toward blue. That shift is the whole thing: pine needles carry a waxy cuticle that scatters cool light, which is why they read colder than almost any other foliage. Saturation stays at 30 because real needles are never vivid, and lightness at 20 because pine is a shade you see in mass, not in a single leaf.

The three below are what people reach for and why they are wrong. Crayola's official Pine Green is teal. Hunter green pulls yellow and reads like a golf course. Darkslategray has given up on being green at all.

None of the dark ones can carry text on #161616. If pine needs to be legible rather than structural, #83B99E is the same hue lifted to 62% and clears AA at 8.1:1.
